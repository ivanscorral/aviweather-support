Frequently asked questions

Where does the weather data come from?
The Aviation Weather Center (NOAA/NWS) Data API.

Why is a report marked Aging or Stale?
METARs are usually issued every 30–60 minutes. Reports over 60 minutes old are marked Aging, and over 90 minutes Stale.

Some groups are listed as "Not decoded". Is that an error?
No. AviWeather lists every group it doesn't interpret yet, so the summary is never mistaken for the full report. The complete raw METAR is always available.

Does it work offline?
Yes. The last successful report for each station is kept on your device and shown with its age.

Important

AviWeather is for situational awareness only. It is not an official weather briefing. Always consult official sources before flight.
